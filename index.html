How to survive

← → or A / D — move
Space / W / ↑ — jump (buy Double Jump in Upgrades for a second one)
Standing on a moving platform carries you with it — don't step off mid-air.
Turret platforms are drawn as their real Alien TD tower — watch the charge glow and dodge.
Gold coins bank permanently. Floating icons are power-ups: shield, speed, high jump, or an extra life.
Levels start gentle and get harder in order — the plasma is slow on Level 1 of World 1 and fastest near the top of World 10.
